# Blockframe Privacy Policy

*Last updated September 2026*

Blockframe is a wireframe tool. It doesn't want your data, and it collects as little as it can.

## What stays on your device
Your work lives in your own browser or app storage. We never see it. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

## What leaves your device
- **Saved frames.** If you save a frame to share it, that frame is stored on Supabase.
- **Site analytics.** The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

## What we never do
No ads. No selling data. No tracking cookies.

## Kids
Blockframe isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them.
